Materials

Everything the Workshop and the suits consume. Base materials drop every cycle; tier materials need the matching suit; combination drops need two or more.

How it works

The whole loop on one page. Every number here mirrors the live server.

1

Work a zone

Wear the Ring, pick a profession and you gather that zone's base material every 3-second cycle: Wood at the Lumber Camp, Rock at the Quarry, Meat at the Hunting Grounds, Bone at the Skeleton Yard. Keep working while you are away and the server pays you out when you come back.

2

Bank and refine

Your backpack fills up; tap the bag and it is banked. From the Bank you sell base materials for coins (Wood and Rock 2, Meat and Bone 3). The Workshop refines 10 base into 1 Plank, Brick, Jerky or Bonedust, which sell for 30 to 40 and are what suits are made of.

3

Suits are keys

Craft the L1 suit for your profession (Lv 10). Wear it and a new, rarer material starts dropping in that zone, which the L2 suit is made of, and so on up to L4. Suits never speed you up; they unlock what can drop. Already own a suit from the Marketplace? Wear it and keep going.

4

Rare drops are steady

Tier materials come at a fixed pace: 1 in 40 cycles for Tier 1, then 1 in 120, 300 and 800. Every cycle adds progress toward the next one, so the rates are a floor, not a lottery. Each rare drop has a 5% chance to arrive doubled; your guild's Prospector tech lifts that to 25%.

5

Professions overlap

Some materials only drop while you wear suits from two professions at once, and Antrom Essence and the Guildstone Shard need all four. The Skeleton Yard feeds everyone: Fang Powder, Skull Charm and Wraith Ash go into the L2, L3 and L4 suits of every profession.

6

Assassin is bought, not crafted

The Ether Assassin pieces are sold in the Decentraland Marketplace. Wearing them unlocks the Skeleton Yard tiers exactly like crafted suits unlock theirs. Played Antrom? Beast Pelts you earned in the Wild Lands are credited here too, and you keep them in Antrom.

What multiplies your gathering

Bonuses stack multiplicatively on the base of 1 item per cycle. Whole items are paid out; fractions carry over to the next cycle.

Ration×1.5 for 10 minutes. 5 Berries or 20 Jerky in the Shop.
Tools+10% per tier, five tiers (Lv 1, 10, 20, 30, 40). Crafted at the Tool Bench from refined materials.
Profession level+5% at Lv 5, 15, 25 … 95. Up to +50%.
Guild HQ+0.5% to every zone per HQ level. HQ 100 is +50% for every member.
Guild techWood Boost and Stone Boost +10% per level, Meat Boost +4%, Loot Luck +2% Bone and +10 backpack. XP Boost, Craft Discount, Merchant and more unlock as the HQ grows.
