Guildstone

Antrom AFK — every wearable you can earn, and what it takes

How it works

  • Put on your Guildstone Ring and work in a zone. You collect that zone's base material every few seconds.
  • Take base materials to the Workshop to refine them. Refined materials are what suits are made of.
  • Craft the L1 suit for your profession. Wear it and a new, rarer material starts dropping in that zone. That material is what the L2 suit is made of, and so on up to L4.
  • Suits do not make you gather faster. They are keys. Rare materials come at a fixed pace per work cycle no matter what you wear or how strong your guild is, so time is the only thing that makes them.
  • Some materials only drop while you wear suits from two professions at once, and a few need all four. You cannot finish one profession without working on the others.
  • Rare drops are steady, not lucky. Every work cycle adds a little progress toward the next one, so the rates you see here are what you will actually get.
  • Only base materials can be sold for coins. Everything else is for crafting. Every suit, Relic and the Crown also costs coins, and the price climbs fast.
  • The Skeleton Yard feeds everyone. Fang Powder, Skull Charm and Wraith Ash go into the L2, L3 and L4 suits of every profession, so every player needs the Assassin pieces eventually.
  • Assassin is different: the Ether Assassin pieces are bought in the Decentraland Marketplace, not crafted. Wear them and the Skeleton Yard unlocks the same way.
  • If you already own any suit from the marketplace, you do not have to craft it. Wear it and keep going.
  • Played Antrom? Beast Pelts you earned in the Wild Lands are given to you here as well, and you keep them in Antrom.
