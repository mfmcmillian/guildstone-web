Antrom AFK — every wearable you can earn, and what it takes

How long the road is

One player, one profession at a time. Away progress counts, so "hours per day" is how long the ring is working for you, not how long you are at the keyboard.

    How gates work

    • Wearing a suit of tier N makes the tier N+1 raw drop appear in that profession's zone. Nothing is shown before then.
    • Combination drops need two suits (or all four). They roll in both zones so you keep earning them whichever of the two you work.
    • Rare drops accumulate as a fraction every cycle on the server — no luck, no streaks. The listed rate is exact on average.
    • Base materials sell for coins at the Depot. Everything above T0 is craft-only.
    • Suits you already own from the marketplace count. You skip that craft and the next tier's drop is already unlocked.
