How to play

Five things to know. The rest you learn by playing.

1

Wear the Ring, pick a zone

The Guildstone Ring is a smart wearable, so the game runs wherever you are in Decentraland. Pick a profession and you gather that zone's material every few seconds, even while you're away.

2

Bank it, refine it

Tap the bag to bank what you carry. Sell base materials for coins, or refine them at the Workshop into the materials suits are made of.

3

Suits are keys

Craft your L1 suit and a rarer material starts dropping in that zone. That material makes the L2 suit, and so on to L4. Suits never speed you up; they unlock what can drop. Already own one? Wear it.

4

No road stands alone

Some materials only drop while you wear suits from two professions at once, and the rarest need all four. Assassin suits aren't crafted: buy the Ether Assassin pieces in the Marketplace and the Skeleton Yard opens the same way.

5

Grow with your guild

Join or found a guild. Donations raise the HQ and every member gathers faster. Rations, better tools and your own profession level stack on top.
